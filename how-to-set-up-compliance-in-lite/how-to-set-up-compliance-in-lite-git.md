Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) tracks your Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) and franchising states as part of your **Getting Started** page's required setup checklist. Three items cover compliance: uploading your FDD, marking your franchising states, and preparing your FDD for e-signature.

All three open the same **Compliance** page, reached from your checklist or from **Compliance** in your sidebar.

> **Prerequisite:** You need Sales write permission to complete these steps.

### Upload your FDD

Select **Continue** next to **Upload your FDD** on your checklist.

On the **States** tab, select a state where you sell franchises. Its detail panel opens.

In the **Franchise Disclosure Document** card, select **Select Document**. Upload a new FDD PDF, or pick one you've already added to your library.

![Franchise Disclosure Document card in a state's detail panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-up-compliance-in-lite/compliance-state-fdd-card.png)
### Mark your franchising states

Back on the **States** tab, check the box next to every state where you actively sell franchises.

With at least one state checked, a bar appears at the bottom of the list. Select **Set Status**, then choose **Actively Franchising**.

FS Ai only tracks compliance deadlines and FDD coverage for states marked this way.

![Set Status menu open over two selected states](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-up-compliance-in-lite/compliance-set-status-menu.png)
### Prepare your FDD for e-signature

Select the **FDDs** tab, then select your FDD in the **Active FDDs** list to expand it.

Select the pencil icon to **Prepare eSignature**. This opens the field builder, where you place signature fields on the document.

Once it's prepared, select the flag icon to **Attach states**, and link the FDD to every state you marked as actively franchising. For the full FDD workflow, including sending FDDs to applicants and leads, see *[How to Manage FDDs]*.

![Expanded FDD row showing the Prepare eSignature and Attach states icons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-up-compliance-in-lite/compliance-fdd-action-icons.png)
Once all three items show **Done**, applicants in your marked states can be shown your FDD and asked to sign it.
