The command palette is a search box in the top bar of Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). Use it to jump to a page or open a record without navigating there yourself.

![The FS Ai top bar with the Search box and the Cmd+K hint, cursor on the search box](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/how-to-search-and-jump-anywhere-with-cmd-k/cmdk-topbar-search.png)
### Opening the palette

Press **Cmd+K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows. The same shortcut closes it again, and so does **Esc** or a click outside it. You can also click the **Search** box in the top bar.

The search field is ready as soon as the palette opens, so you can start typing straight away.

### Jumping to a page

With the search box empty, the palette lists your main pages under **Pages**, five at a time: Home, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Locations, Vendors, Brand Overview, Applicant Portal, Library, and Settings.

![The command palette open with an empty search box, showing the Pages list: Home, Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Locations](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/how-to-search-and-jump-anywhere-with-cmd-k/cmdk-pages-list.png)
Typing narrows this list, and it matches on more than the page name. Type "deals" and Sales comes up. Type "files" and Library comes up.

### Searching your records

Type at least two characters and FS Ai searches across every brand you have access to, then groups what it finds by type: Prospects, Leads, Franchisees, Locations, Vendors, Franchisee Entities, Deals, Tasks, Projects, Team Members, Courses, Territories, Tracking Links, Assets, and Helpdesk Articles.

![The command palette with denver typed, showing results grouped under Leads, Locations, and Territories](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/how-to-search-and-jump-anywhere-with-cmd-k/cmdk-search-results.png)
Each group shows up to five matches, with a second line for context: a lead's email address, a location's street address, the applicant behind a deal. You only see records you already have permission to open.

### Opening a result

Select a result and it opens where it normally lives. Most records open in a panel over the page you were already on, so you keep your place. An asset opens in the full-screen preview, a course opens in the course builder, and an operations manual opens in its editor.

![A location opened from the command palette, shown as a panel over the Home page](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/how-to-search-and-jump-anywhere-with-cmd-k/cmdk-result-panel.png)
### Recent items

Records you open from the palette are remembered. Next time you open it with an empty search box, they sit under **Recent**, newest first, up to five of them.

That list lives in the browser you're using, and entries drop off after two days.

### Keyboard tips

- **Up and down arrows**: move through the results
- **Enter**: open the highlighted result
- **Esc**: close the palette

The same three hints sit along the bottom edge of the palette.
