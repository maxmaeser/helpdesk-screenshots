The Brand page is where you manage a specific brand's public identity: its story, its news, its visual style, and how it presents itself.

This is different from the concept of a brand in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). *[What Is a Brand?]* explains how brands relate to your organization. This article covers the **Brand** page itself, the sidebar item where you edit and view that content.

Go to **Brand** in the sidebar. The page has six tabs: **Overview**, **Feed**, **News**, **Identity**, **Voice & style**, and **Gallery**.

### Overview tab

This is the brand's profile at a glance. It includes:

**About Brand**: a description of the brand.

**Leadership**: the people who lead the brand.

**Headquarters**: the brand's city and state.

**Network map**: a map of the brand's locations.

![Brand Overview tab showing About Brand, Locations, At a Glance metrics, and the Headquarters and Network map sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-overview.png)

### Feed tab

The Feed shows posts published to the brand, shown as a scrolling card grid.

If you have permission to manage the feed, click into **Manage Posts** to create, edit, and publish posts.

![Brand Feed tab showing a published post card and the Manage Posts button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-feed.png)

### News tab

News tracks coverage and mentions of the brand. Each item has a headline, summary, source, and publish date.

You can tag a news item by kind (National, Trade, Press, Blog, Review, or Social), sentiment (Positive, Neutral, Watch, or Negative), and scope (brand-wide, or tied to a specific location).

Click **Add news** and paste a link to auto-fill the headline, summary, and image where available.

![Brand News tab showing the empty Press & News state with the Add news button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-news.png)

### Identity tab

Identity holds the brand's style guide: logo, colors, and fonts, including custom font uploads.

Below the style guide, **Identity Guidelines** documents the logo, color, and type rules for the network.

![Brand Identity tab showing the Full Logo and Small Logo cards plus the color palette swatches](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-identity.png)

### Voice & style tab

This tab holds the brand's voice and story settings.

Below that, **Voice & Style Guidelines** documents how the brand should sound across channels.

![Brand Voice and style tab showing the Voice Of The Brand card and the Voice and Style Guidelines subsections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-voice-style.png)

### Gallery tab

Gallery holds the brand's mockup assets, images and renders used to represent the brand.

![Brand Gallery tab showing the asset grid grouped by category, starting with Logos](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-brand-page/brand-gallery.png)

### Editing permissions

Editing any section of the Brand page requires the Workspace Settings permission for that brand. Without it, you can view the page but not make changes. See *[Understanding Roles and Permissions]* for how permissions work.
