Quick views narrow your funnel leads to groups such as unassigned contacts or people with a signed FDD. Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) applies the matching filters to your leads table.

These shortcuts appear for brands using the Recruitment Funnel. The Applicant Portal version of the leads table uses the regular filter controls.

### Apply a quick view

1. Open **Pipeline** under **Sales**. In FranDev (Recruitment Funnel), open **Leads** from the sidebar.
2. Hover over a quick-view chip above the table to see its name, then click one such as **Unassigned**.
3. Review the matching leads. Click the selected chip again to remove that quick view.

![Funnel leads with the Unassigned quick view selected](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/leads-quick-views/leads-quick-views-selected.png)

### What each view includes

- **Needs FDD approval**: leads whose FDD request is waiting for review.
- **Unassigned**: leads without an assigned agent.
- **New this week**: leads added within the last seven days, rather than the current calendar week.
- **Signed FDD**: leads recorded as having signed their FDD.
- **Agreement locked**: leads whose recorded agreement unlock date is tomorrow or later.
- **Stale**: leads whose last recorded activity was at least 14 days ago.

### Combine or remove quick views

You can select more than one chip. The table narrows to leads matching the selected criteria together.

![Funnel leads with Unassigned and New this week selected](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/leads-quick-views/leads-quick-views-combined.png)

Your other filters stay in place when you change quick views. If the table is empty, check both your selected chips and your regular filters.

Removing a chip removes its matching filter; it does not change any lead records. For other filters and saved views, see *[How to Filter, Sort, and Search Your Contacts]*.
