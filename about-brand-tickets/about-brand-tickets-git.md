Tickets is where support requests from your franchisees land inside Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai).

When a franchisee submits a ticket to your brand, it appears here for your team to see and work.

### Where to find it

Go to **Operations > Tickets**.

If no franchisee has submitted a ticket yet, the page shows an empty state: "No tickets yet." Franchisees' tickets to this brand appear here once submitted.

![Tickets page showing the No tickets yet empty state](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-brand-tickets/tickets-empty-state.png)

### Reading the tickets table

Each ticket is a row with these columns:

**Subject**: what the franchisee's ticket is about.

**Submitted by**: which franchisee raised it.

**Status**: where the ticket stands, for example "In progress".

**Priority**: how urgent it is, for example "Normal".

**Category**: what area the ticket falls under, for example "Real Estate".

**Assignee**: who on your team is handling it.

**Updated**: when the ticket last changed.

![Tickets table with subject, status, priority, category, assignee, and updated columns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-brand-tickets/tickets-table.png)

### What this gives you

The Tickets table is your single view of every support request franchisees have raised with your brand, with enough at a glance to see what's open, how urgent it is, and who owns it.
