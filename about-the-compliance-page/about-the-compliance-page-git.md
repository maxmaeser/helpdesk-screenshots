The Compliance page is where Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) tracks where you are allowed to sell franchises, which Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) covers each state, and what is coming up for renewal.

It is one screen: a working panel on the left, and a live map of the United States and Canada on the right.

> **Prerequisite:** You need Sales read permission to open the page, and Sales write permission on the brand to change anything on it.

### Where to find it

Go to **Sales → Compliance**.

The page shows one brand at a time. If you have access to more than one, a brand switcher sits at the top left.

### How the page is laid out

Across the top: your brand, the page title, and an orange **N issues to resolve** chip. Below that, a row of tabs. Below that, the panel and the map.

The map never goes away. Whichever tab you are on, it stays on the right, shaded by state. Green means you are actively franchising there and clear to disclose. Orange means you are franchising there but something is missing. States you have not marked stay unshaded.

Selecting a state, either from a list row or on the map itself, opens that state's detail panel. Right-clicking a state on the map opens a shortcut menu for the same actions.

### The tabs

**States**: every state and province, with your franchising status and FDD coverage for each. This is the tab the page opens on.

**FDDs**: your FDD documents, each one expandable to the states attached to it. A needs-attention band at the top calls out active states with no FDD, and documents not yet prepared for e-signature. Documents in your library that are not attached to any state are listed underneath, under **From Your Library**.

![The FDDs tab, with the needs-attention band above the active FDD and its Prepared badge](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/about-the-compliance-page/compliance-fdds-tab.png)

**Deadlines**: registration expirations and FDD renewal dates for every state that has one. They group under **Overdue** first, then by calendar month. Each row shows the date and how long is left, and opens that state when selected.

![The Deadlines tab, with two registrations grouped under Overdue](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/about-the-compliance-page/compliance-deadlines-tab.png)

The page remembers which tab you were last on.

### The issues chip

The orange chip counts two things together: active states with no FDD, and FDD documents that have not been prepared for e-signature yet.

Selecting it takes you to the FDDs tab, where both are itemized with the state or document named. The same number sits on the FDDs tab itself. When the count is zero, the chip is hidden.

### Registration status

Every state row carries a registration status badge. There are six:

- **Registered**: filed and registered with the state
- **Filed**: filed, but the registration is not complete
- **Filing Only**: the state requires a filing, not a registration
- **Registration Required**: the state requires a registration you do not have on file
- **Expired**: the registration date on file has passed
- **No Registration**: the state requires neither

What each status means for your filing obligations, and how to update a state's filing status, are covered in *[How to Manage FDDs]*.

### Narrowing the list

Above the state list sit a search box and a **Select All** checkbox, then five controls:

- **Country**: United States, Canada, US Territories. Pick any combination
- **Sort**: Active first, Name A-Z, Expiry soonest, Needs attention
- **Franchising**: All states, Franchising, Not franchising
- **Status**: any combination of the six registration statuses
- **FDD**: Any FDD, Has FDD, No FDD

These narrow the list only. The map stays whole, so you keep the full picture while you work through a subset.

If a combination leaves nothing, the list offers a **Reset filters** button.

Checking one or more states raises a bar at the bottom of the panel with **Set Status** and **Assign FDD**.
