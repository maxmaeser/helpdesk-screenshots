Your profile holds the personal details that identify you across Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai).

This information appears in communications sent on your behalf, in task assignments, and when colleagues view your account. You can edit it at any time.

### Opening your profile

1. Click your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings**.
2. Under the **Account** group, open the **Profile** page.

![Profile menu open at the bottom of the sidebar with Settings highlighted](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-menu-settings.png)
The Profile page is organized into tabs: **Details**, **Organizations**, **Sales Assignability**, **Preferences**, and **Security**.

![Settings page with the Account, Organization, and Brand groups in the left navigation, and the Profile page open on the Details tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/settings-scopes-nav.png)
### Details

The **Details** tab holds your core profile fields:

- **First name** and **Last name**: how your name appears throughout the platform.
- **Email**: the address tied to your account. This is set during sign-up and cannot be changed here.
- **Phone**: your contact number.
- **LinkedIn URL**: a link to your LinkedIn profile.
- **Primary job title**: your role or position (for example, "Franchise Development Manager").
- **Profile picture**: your avatar in the sidebar, chat, assignments, and team views. Click your avatar to upload a new image or remove the current one.

Edit any field by clicking it, making your change, and clicking away. Changes save automatically.

![Details tab showing the profile photo uploader, First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, LinkedIn, and Job Title fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-details-v2.png)
### Organizations

The **Organizations** tab shows which organizations you belong to, and lets you join or create a new one.

![Organizations tab listing the organizations the agent belongs to, with their role badge and a Join button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-organizations.png)
### Sales Assignability

The **Sales Assignability** tab controls how you receive leads from the applicant portal. For each brand you have access to, three radio buttons set your assignment:

- **Automatic**: routes new leads to you.
- **Manual**: lets teammates choose you when booking.
- **Off**: keeps you out of lead assignment.

![Sales Assignability tab listing each brand with Automatic, Manual, and Off options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-assignability-v2.png)
### Preferences

The **Preferences** tab sets your **time format** (12-hour or 24-hour) and your **theme** (System, Light, or Dark).

![Preferences tab with Time notation and Theme controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-preferences.png)
### Security

The **Security** tab is where you protect your account. Here you can change your password, turn **two-factor authentication** on or off, and manage your personal **API key** for developer integrations.

![Security tab showing the password change form and a list of active sessions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-v2.png)
Under **Active sessions**, you can see every device currently signed in to your account.

Each row shows the browser, operating system, location, and sign-in time. Your current session is marked **This device**.

To sign out one device, click **Sign out** next to it. To sign out everywhere except your current device, click **Sign out all others**.

![Active sessions card with the Sign out all others button in the header and a Sign out button on a non-current session row](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-sessions-actions.png)
Changing your password also signs out all your other sessions automatically.

![Security tab showing the two-factor authentication toggle and the personal API key field](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-api.png)
### Turning on two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second step to signing in, using time-based codes from an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password.

1. On the **Security** tab, click **Enable** next to Two-factor authentication, then click **Get Started**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, enter the secret key shown below the code manually instead.

![Scan QR Code step of the two-factor authentication setup window, showing the QR code and manual entry key](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-setup-qr.png)
3. Click **Continue**, then enter the 6-digit code your app generates and click **Verify**.
4. Save your backup codes somewhere safe. Each one works only once, and gets you back into your account if you ever lose access to your authenticator app.

![Backup codes step of the two-factor authentication setup window, showing 10 generated codes and a Copy all codes link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-backup-codes.png)
5. Click **I've Saved My Codes** to finish.

The Security tab now shows two-factor authentication as enabled, along with how many backup codes you have left.

![Security tab with two-factor authentication enabled, showing the Disable button and backup codes remaining count](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-enabled.png)
### Turning off two-factor authentication

Click **Disable** next to Two-factor authentication, enter a current code from your authenticator app, and click **Disable** again to confirm.

![Disable confirmation dialog for two-factor authentication, showing the 6-digit code input](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-disable.png)
If your organization requires two-factor authentication, the **Disable** button is not available and you cannot turn it off.

### Regenerating backup codes

If you're running low on backup codes, click **Regenerate backup codes**, enter a current code from your authenticator app, and click **Regenerate**.

This invalidates all of your existing backup codes and issues a new set of 10.

![Regenerate backup codes form on the Security tab, showing the code input and Regenerate button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/setting-up-your-profile/profile-security-2fa-regenerate.png)
### Connecting your calendar

Your calendar connection and booking events live on a separate page.

In your settings, under the **Account** group, open **Calendar & Scheduling**. See *[How to Connect Your Calendar]* for the full walkthrough.

### Why your profile matters

Your profile shows up in several places:

- **Applicant communications**: when emails or messages are sent on your behalf, your name and role may appear to the recipient.
- **Task assignments**: team members see your name and avatar when tasks are assigned to or by you.
- **Team views**: organization admins and colleagues see your details in the team management sections.

Keeping your profile current helps your team stay coordinated and ensures applicants receive professional communications with accurate contact information.
