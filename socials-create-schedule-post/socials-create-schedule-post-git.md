Use the social calendar in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) to draft a post, tailor it for each channel, and choose when it goes out.

> You need Socials enabled for a Full OS brand, Marketing write access, and at least one connected social account.

### Open a draft

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Socials**.
2. Click **Create** to open a new draft in the composer. To continue an existing draft, open **Drafts** and select it.
3. Give the post an internal name so your team can recognize it.

![Calendar controls for creating and finding drafts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-create-schedule-post/socials-post-calendar.png)

### Choose accounts and content

1. Use the channel tabs to work on each platform. The plus control, **Add or remove channels**, changes the platforms included in the post.
2. Select the accounts that should receive the post on each channel.
3. Write the **Caption**. Check which account you are editing before adding account-specific wording.
4. Use **Add media** to attach images or video. Review media warnings and the preview for each channel.

![Draft composer with caption, media, and publishing controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-create-schedule-post/socials-post-composer.png)

Platform settings vary. Instagram offers **Post** and **Reel** formats, with **Share to feed** for Reels. A single Instagram video publishes as a Reel.

Media requirements and caption limits depend on the platform. Resolve the warnings shown in the composer before publishing.

![Channel selection for a draft](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-create-schedule-post/socials-post-channels.png)

### Keep a draft or publish

Changes save as you edit. Check the save status before leaving. A draft stays unpublished until you choose a publishing action.

1. Open **Publish**.
2. Choose **Schedule** for later delivery, select a future date and time, and check the timezone. Click **Confirm** to schedule it.
3. Alternatively, choose **Post now** to send it immediately.

These actions publish to the selected social accounts. Review the account selection, caption, and media first.

### Understand the status

A draft is unfinished content. A scheduled post is queued for its chosen time. A posted item has been published.

Publishing can also be processing, partially successful, or failed. Open the post to inspect its individual channel results instead of assuming every account received it.
