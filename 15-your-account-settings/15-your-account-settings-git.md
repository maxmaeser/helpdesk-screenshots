Your account settings hold your profile details and how the portal notifies you. Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings** to get there.

## Profile Details

This is the default tab. It shows your photo, name, email, and phone number.

![Profile Details tab showing name, email, and phone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/15-your-account-settings/account-settings-profile.png)
Click the pencil icon next to **Profile Details** to edit. Your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Phone** become editable fields. Your **Email** stays fixed. It isn't editable here.

![Profile Details in edit mode with Save Changes and Cancel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/15-your-account-settings/account-settings-edit.png)
Make your changes, then press **Save Changes** to keep them. **Cancel** backs out without saving.

Your photo has its own controls above the fields: **Upload New Avatar** swaps it out (PNGs and JPEGs under 5MB), and **Delete Avatar** removes it.

## Notification Preferences

The second tab controls how you hear about activity in the portal: new chat messages, task assignments, reminders and status changes, project updates, vendor and location changes, and new manuals, among others.

![Notification Preferences table with Yes/No toggles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/15-your-account-settings/account-settings-notifications.png)
Each notification has two separate switches: **Notify Me In-App** and **Notify Me By Email**. Set either one to **Yes** or **No** on its own, so you can, for example, keep in-app task reminders on while turning off the email copy.
