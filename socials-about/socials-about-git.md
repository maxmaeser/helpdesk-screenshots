Socials brings social posts, conversations, account connections, and performance into Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). Open **Marketing** → **Socials** to work with the selected brand.

> Socials is available for Full OS brands with the feature enabled. Your brand access and permissions determine which accounts and posts you can view or change.

### The four tabs

- **Calendar**: plan content, open drafts, and see scheduled and posted content. This is the starting view.
- **Inbox**: read and respond to Instagram and Facebook direct messages and comments.
- **Analytics**: review performance for the selected period, channels, and accounts.
- **Accounts**: view connected channels, their status, and the people or locations they belong to.

![Socials calendar and four navigation tabs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-about/socials-about-tabs.png)

### Start with an account

Connect a social account before creating a post. Without an account, **Create** is disabled and the accounts view prompts you to connect your first channel.

An account connection makes publishing possible. Posts, messages, and performance figures appear as content and activity become available for that account.

![Accounts view with the first-channel prompt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-about/socials-about-accounts.png)

### Who sees what

Brand staff with Marketing read access can view brand Socials. Publishing and changes require Marketing write access.

Franchisees have a separate portal view for their accounts and locations. Their page has **Calendar**, **Analytics**, and **Accounts**. Their location's Socials tab narrows content to that unit.

A franchisee may see brand-created content for a linked account without being able to change the whole post. The composer explains any editing restrictions.

For the connection steps, see *[How to Connect Your Social Accounts]*. For drafting and publishing, see *[How to Create and Schedule a Social Post]*.
