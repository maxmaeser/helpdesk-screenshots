Vendors on Operations is where your brand keeps one approved list of outside suppliers and partners for franchisees to order from: food, packaging, tech, services, anything that comes from outside the four walls of a unit.

What you set here, usage requirements and categories, is what franchisees see in their own **Vendors** page in the portal.

### Where to find it

Go to **Operations → Vendors**. It opens on the **Vendors** tab.

### The vendor list

The main view is a table. Each row shows the vendor name, a **Usage** badge, its **Category**, how many locations it is assigned to, and contact info: email and phone.

Search vendors from the search bar, filter and sort from the icons beside it, and switch between saved table **Views**.

![Operations Vendors tab showing the vendor table with Usage and Category columns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-manage-your-vendor-list/vendors-tab-list.png)
### Add a vendor

Open **Actions** and select **Create Vendor**.

You get two setup paths:

- **Quick Setup**: enter the vendor's website and Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) fetches their logo, name, and description automatically.
- **Manual Entry**: enter every field yourself.

Either path lands on the same form. **Vendor Name** and at least one **Category** are required.

Email, phone, and website are optional, along with a short description and a note on the products or services the vendor provides.

![Vendor Details form with name, categories, usage pills, and contact fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-manage-your-vendor-list/vendors-create-vendor-form.png)
### Set usage and categories

**Usage** tells franchisees how strongly your brand backs a vendor. Pick one:

- **Required**: franchisees must use this vendor.
- **Recommended**: your brand's preferred choice, not mandatory.
- **Optional**: an approved alternative.

**Categories** group vendors by what they provide, for example Food Supplier or Marketing Agency. A vendor needs at least one.

If the category you need does not exist yet, create it from the same picker.

You can change either one later from the vendor's row in the table or from its detail panel, without opening the full edit form.

### The vendor detail panel

Click a vendor row to open its detail panel. It has five tabs:

- **Details**: the vendor record, including the fields from the create form.
- **Contacts**: the account managers or reps to reach at this vendor.
- **Locations Assignments**: which of your locations are assigned this vendor.
- **Manuals**: manuals linked to this vendor.
- **Assets**: files tied to this vendor.

![Vendor detail panel open on the Details tab, showing the tab bar and Usage badge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-manage-your-vendor-list/vendor-detail-panel-tabs.png)
### Assign vendors to locations in bulk

Assigning vendors to locations one at a time does not scale past a handful of units. The **Assignment Templates** tab groups a set of vendors together so you can apply the whole group to many locations at once.

Select **Create Assignment Template**, name it, and add the vendors it should include. From a location's vendor list, use **Apply Assignment Template** to assign every vendor in the template in one action.

![Assignment Templates tab with Name, Vendors Assigned, and Created At columns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-manage-your-vendor-list/vendor-assignment-templates-tab.png)
Franchisees see the results of all of this on their own read-only *[Vendors]* page: the same usage badges, categories, and vendor details you set here.
