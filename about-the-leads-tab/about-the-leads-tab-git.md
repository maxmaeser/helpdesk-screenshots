If your brand runs a funnel instead of an applicant portal, **Sales → Analytics** looks different from the standard Overview page.

Funnel brands land on a **Leads** tab instead, built around the outcomes your funnel produces: leads coming in, where they land, and how far they get toward becoming franchisees.

For the portal version of this page, see *[About Sales Analytics]*. For how leads enter your funnel in the first place, see *[About the Funnel Builder]*.

### Who sees this tab

Analytics composes its tabs per brand. Brands running a funnel (built in **Studio → Funnel**) see **Leads** and **Emails**. Brands with a standard applicant portal see **Overview**, **Portal Steps**, and **Emails** instead.

Switch brands with the brand selector at the top of the page to check which set you get. This selector is independent of the one on **Sales → Pipeline**.

### Setting your range

Use the **View** dropdown to switch between **Daily View**, **Weekly View**, and **Monthly View**, and the **From** and **To** fields to set the date range. Every chart on the Leads tab respects these settings.

The Leads tab doesn't include a **Generate Report** button or a **Generate Insights** panel. Those are specific to the Overview tab on portal brands.

### Reading the charts

- **Leads Over Time**: new leads across your date range, with a **Bar** and **Line** toggle.
- **Leads By State**: lead volume by state. Toggle between a **Map** view and a **List** view, which shows a plain table of state name and lead count.
- **Leads By Source**: a donut chart plus a table breaking leads down by source, with counts and percentages. The **Include Organic Leads?** toggle controls whether leads with no attributed source are counted. Turn it off and any leads without a campaign or UTM source drop out of the chart and table entirely.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-the-leads-tab/leads-by-source.png)

- **FDDs Signed**: Franchise Disclosure Documents signed by leads in your funnel, with the same Bar/Line toggle.
- **Calls Booked**: intro calls booked through your funnel's Book a Call step.
