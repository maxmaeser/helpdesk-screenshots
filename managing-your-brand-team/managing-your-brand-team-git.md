The Team & Access page under Brand in your settings shows who's on a specific brand's team, along with their sales assignability, the brand's external contacts, and franchisee invite approval.

This is different from organization access level, which controls what a person can actually see and do across Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). That's covered in *[Understanding Roles and Permissions]*. This article is about brand-level team settings, not permissions.

To get there, click your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar, select **Settings**, and open the **Team & Access** page under Brand.

### Brand team

The Brand team list shows everyone with access to this brand: their name, avatar, and a calendar-connected indicator if they've linked Google Calendar or Office 365.

Members show an **Auto Assigned** or **Manually Assignable** badge based on their sales assignability setting.

Click a team member's row to open their profile. There's no invite button on this page. Adding someone to your organization happens at the organization level. See *[How to Invite Users]*.

### Sales assignability

This section controls which team members can be assigned to Applicant Portal leads for this brand.

Each member has a setting of **Automatic**, **Manual**, or **Off**. Automatic routes new leads to them. Manual lets teammates choose them when booking. Off keeps them out of lead assignment entirely.

See *[Auto and Manual Lead Assignment]* for more on what each option controls.

![Sales assignability card showing the Automatic, Manual, and Off segmented control for each team member](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/managing-your-brand-team/sales-assignability.png)
### Contacts

Contacts are external people associated with the brand, not FS Ai users. They can be assigned to projects and tasks.

Click **Add contact** to create one with a name, email, phone number, and notes.

Use the search box to find a contact by name or email. Each contact has an actions menu with **Edit** and **Remove**.

![Contacts card showing the search box, an existing contact, and the Add contact button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/managing-your-brand-team/contacts-card.png)
### Franchisee invite approvals

This toggle controls whether franchisee team invites need your review before they go out.

Turn on **Require approval for franchisee team invites** to review each invite before it's sent. Turn it off to let franchisee team invites send immediately.

![Franchisee invite approvals card with the Require Approval For Franchisee Team Invites toggle switched on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/managing-your-brand-team/franchisee-invite-approvals.png)
