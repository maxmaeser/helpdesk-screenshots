Add your portal to your phone's home screen to open it from your brand's icon. It opens in its own window, without the browser bars.

## Open the install instructions

Tap **Add** on the **Add to Home Screen** banner in your portal.

![Portal home with the Add to Home Screen banner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-add-to-home-screen/fp-add-to-home-screen-banner.png)
If you dismissed the banner, open your avatar menu and select **Add to Home Screen**.

![Avatar menu with Add to Home Screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-add-to-home-screen/fp-add-to-home-screen-menu.png)
## iPhone or iPad

1. Open your portal in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button in Safari's toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** to confirm.

![Portal instructions for adding the portal in Safari](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-add-to-home-screen/fp-add-to-home-screen-ios.png)
## Android

1. Open your portal in Chrome.
2. Open Chrome's three-dot menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**, and confirm.

If the portal's install dialog shows **Install now**, tap it and confirm the browser's installation prompt instead.

![Install dialog when the browser offers direct installation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-add-to-home-screen/fp-add-to-home-screen-install.png)
The manual Chrome instructions are also available when a direct install prompt is unavailable.

![Portal instructions for adding the portal in Chrome](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-add-to-home-screen/fp-add-to-home-screen-android.png)
## Open your installed portal

Tap your brand's icon on your home screen to return to the portal.

The banner stays hidden inside the installed app. Closing the banner once hides it for five days; closing it a second time hides it permanently in that browser.
