Socials brings your connected accounts, posts, and performance together across your locations. It appears in your portal when your brand enables it.

## Your Socials page

Open **Socials** in the sidebar. The page has three tabs:

- **Calendar**: view drafts, scheduled posts, and published content for your accounts.
- **Analytics**: review the performance data available for your accounts.
- **Accounts**: see connected accounts, linked locations, and connections that need attention.

![Your Socials page and its three tabs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-socials/fp-socials-overview.png)

You need a connected account before creating a post. If **Create** is unavailable, check the accounts view with your franchise team.

The accounts view shows which locations an account is linked to. Those links help determine where its posts appear in the portal.

![Your social accounts and location links](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-socials/fp-socials-accounts.png)

## Socials for one unit

Open **Locations**, choose a unit, and select its **Socials** tab. If you operate one unit, **Location** opens that unit directly.

This view focuses the calendar on the selected location. Use **Manage connections** to open its **Connections** tab and review the linked accounts.

![A location's Socials tab and connection shortcut](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-socials/fp-socials-location.png)

## Editing posts

Your available controls depend on the post's ownership and status. You can work on posts your team created while they remain editable.

Brand-created posts may allow account-specific caption changes while keeping shared content under your brand's control. Read the notice in the composer before editing.

Check the selected accounts and preview before publishing. Scheduled or posted content has different editing controls from a draft.
