Finder is a lead-sourcing tool in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). It searches for prospects that match criteria you set, then lets you pull the ones you want into your audience.

Finder carries a purple **Beta** badge in the sidebar. It's an actively developing feature, so expect it to change.

### Where to find it

Go to **Marketing → Finder**.

The list page shows your **Total Searches** count and a table of every search you've run: Title, Status, Query, Matches, and Created.

Click **New Search** to start a new one.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-finder/finder-list.png)
### Opening a search

Click a search in the table to open its results.

The header shows the search title and a summary reading how many results were matched against how many you've reviewed, for example "42 matched / 10 reviewed".

Two filters narrow the results: **Has email** and **Only matched**.

An **Actions** menu and a **Review & import** button sit alongside the filters.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-finder/finder-search-open.png)
### Reading the results table

Each result row includes: Name, Status, Criteria, Email, Position, Location, URL, and Created.

Results come in two kinds:

**Matched**: results that meet your search criteria.

**Unmatched**: results that were sourced but didn't meet your criteria.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-finder/finder-results-table.png)
### Getting results into your audience

Results in Finder aren't leads yet. They're sourced data waiting for you to decide what to do with them.

Click **Review & import** to bring matched results into FS Ai as prospects.

Once imported, they follow the same audience and lead workflow as any other contact. See *[How to Import Audiences]* for what happens during import, and *[General Definitions]* for how a prospect differs from a lead.
