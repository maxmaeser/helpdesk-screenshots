The Creative Editor is where you build designs and slide decks for branded presentations in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai).

> **Prerequisite:** Creative Editor requires Full OS and must be enabled for your account. It isn't available on FranDev (Recruitment Funnel).

### Where to find it

Go to **Studio → Creative Editor**. The page has **Designs** and **Templates** tabs.

If your brand has no designs yet, the page shows an empty state: "No Designs Yet." Create your first design to start building branded presentations.

A **New Design** button appears both in the toolbar and in the empty state.

![Sliders page showing the No Sliders Yet empty state with New Slider button](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/about-sliders/sliders-empty-state.png)

### Creating a deck

Click **New Design** to open the editor.

Use the **Design name** field to name your design. Click **Save** to save your work. Closing any other way while you have unsaved changes prompts you to confirm you want to lose them.

Click **Export** to choose an export format. Saved designs can also be opened from the asset library and shared through their asset permissions.

From here you build out your branded presentation deck within FS Ai, rather than assembling slides in a separate tool.
