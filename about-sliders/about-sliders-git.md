Sliders is where you build slide decks for branded presentations in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai).

> **Prerequisite:** Sliders requires a full plan. It isn't available on FS Ai Lite.

### Where to find it

Go to **Studio > Sliders**.

If your brand has no decks yet, the page shows an empty state: "No Sliders Yet." Create your first slider deck to start building branded presentations.

A **New Slider** button appears both in the page header and in the center of the empty state.

![Sliders page showing the No Sliders Yet empty state with New Slider button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-sliders/sliders-empty-state.png)

### Creating a deck

Click **New Slider** to open the deck editor.

The editor opens full screen with the deck titled "Untitled Slides." **Save** and **Exit** buttons sit at the top.

From here you build out your branded presentation deck within FS Ai, rather than assembling slides in a separate tool.

![Slider deck editor opened on an Untitled Slides deck](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-sliders/sliders-deck-editor.png)
