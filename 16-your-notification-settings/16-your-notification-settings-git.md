The portal keeps you posted on what's happening in your business: chat messages, task updates, deal changes, course completions, and more. You control which of these reach you, and how, from your notification preferences.

## Where notifications land

Click the bell icon in the top bar to open your notifications. Each one shows what happened, which brand it's tied to, and a link straight to the item. New notifications carry a **New** tag, and you can search or archive them from the same panel.

![Notifications panel open from the bell icon in the top bar, showing a list of task alerts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/16-your-notification-settings/notif-inbox-panel.png)
## Turning notifications on or off

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings**. In the panel on the left, click **Notification Preferences**.

![Settings panel with Profile Details and Notification Preferences links, Notification Preferences highlighted](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/16-your-notification-settings/notif-settings-nav.png)
## What you can control

You'll see a list covering everything from chat messages and task assignments to deal updates, location and vendor changes, and course or certificate milestones.

Each notification type has two independent switches: **Notify Me In-App** and **Notify Me By Email**. Set either one to **Yes** or **No**, and your choice takes effect right away.

In-app and email are controlled separately, so you can get an email for something without an in-app alert, or the other way around.
