Your funnel isn't done once the questions are built. Three items on your **Getting Started** page's setup checklist take it the rest of the way live: adding resources for applicants, turning on your funnel emails, and publishing your funnel.

Publishing is required before your funnel can go live. The other two are recommended, not required, but skipping them means no follow-up emails and nothing on the end screen your applicants finish on.

### Add resources for applicants

Every funnel ends on a **Resources** step, the screen an applicant lands on once their application is done.

Go to **Studio → Funnel** and select **Resources** at the bottom of the Structure rail.

Add a document or video from your Library, or upload one straight from the editor. Each resource shows on the end screen as a card with its type and read or watch time.

![Resources end screen editor with document and video cards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-publish-your-funnel/funnel-resources-end-screen.png)
### Turn on your funnel emails

FS Ai can follow up your leads automatically with email automations built for your funnel.

In the funnel builder, click the **Automations** icon in the top toolbar to open the panel.

If none are set up yet, apply a setup template to create your funnel's email automations, or build your own on the **Marketing → Workflows** page.

Expand a card to read its email and see when it sends. A toggle switches each automation on, but it stays off until the email has content and your sending domain is verified.

![Automations panel in the funnel builder](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-publish-your-funnel/funnel-automations-panel.png)
### Publish your funnel

Click the **Publish** pill in the top right of the funnel builder, then select **Publish now** in the popover.

Publishing is staged into your draft. It only takes effect once you select **Save**.

Once live, the pill reads **Published** with a green dot, and the same popover lets you **Unpublish** if you need to take the funnel down.

![Publish popover showing a live funnel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-publish-your-funnel/funnel-publish-popover.png)
### If your funnel needs a payment method first

Some brands need an active payment method on the organization before their funnel can go live. Everything else in the dashboard stays free to set up either way.

If yours is one of them, the publish popover shows **Add payment to go live** instead of **Publish now**. Select it to go to the **Billing** page under Organization in your settings.

Add a card or a US bank account there. Billing starts once you add a payment method, then continues monthly.

![Add a payment method card on the Billing settings page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-publish-your-funnel/billing-add-payment-method.png)
Once the payment method is added, go back to **Studio → Funnel** and publish as normal.
