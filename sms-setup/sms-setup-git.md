The SMS numbers card in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) manages the numbers your brand texts from and its carrier registration.

> SMS must be enabled for the brand. You need permission to change brand workspace settings.

Texts to US phones do not deliver until carrier registration is approved. Buying a number alone does not make those messages deliverable.

### Buy a number

1. Click your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings**.
2. Under Brand, open **Connections** and find **SMS numbers**. Select the correct brand if you can switch brands.
3. Click **Buy number**. Choose United States or Canada and optionally enter an area code.
4. Click **Continue**, review the displayed monthly cost, and confirm with **Buy number**.

![SMS numbers card before buying a number](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/sms-setup/sms-setup-empty.png)

In a FranDev workspace, the card is on **Studio** → **Email Templates**, under **Domains**.

### Register the brand

1. After a number appears, click **Start registration**.
2. Complete the business details, including entity type, legal identity, contact details, website, and address.
3. Continue to the messaging details. Describe the messages and how recipients opt in, including the form link. Supply two different sample messages and the requested keywords.
4. Continue to review, check the filing and displayed fees, then click **Submit registration**.

![Business details at the start of carrier registration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/sms-setup/sms-setup-business.png)

The review step checks the filing before submission. Submission files the registration for review; it does not mean the brand is approved. Review can take days.

### Answer a change request

Open **Answer reviewer** when it appears on the card. A request may ask for written answers, links, or supporting files.

Respond to each point, then click **Send answers**. For a filing that needs correction, **Edit and resubmit it** reopens the business and messaging details.

![Carrier change request and answer fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/sms-setup/sms-setup-reviewer.png)

If the filing asks for a verification code, enter the code sent to its mobile number and use **Verify**. Follow the status shown until registration is approved.

### Send a test

With an active number and approved registration, click **Send test message**. Enter **Your phone number**, confirm that it is your own number and that you consent, then click **Send test**.

![Test-message form with consent and send action](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/sms-setup/sms-setup-test.png)

This sends a real informational text to that number. It checks delivery separately from the templates you create in Marketing.
