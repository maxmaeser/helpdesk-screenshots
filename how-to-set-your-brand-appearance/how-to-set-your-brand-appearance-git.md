The Appearance page under Brand in your settings controls the logos, favicon, and primary color that represent a brand across Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai): the applicant portal, booking pages, and email headers.

> **Prerequisite:** You need permission to edit brand settings to upload logos or change the primary color. Without it, the upload and save controls are disabled.

To get there, click your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar, select **Settings**, and open the **Appearance** page under Brand.

This is different from the **Identity** tab on the Brand page in the sidebar, which covers a brand's public story and voice. See *[About the Brand Page]* for that.

![Appearance settings page with logo upload rows and the primary color field](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-your-brand-appearance/brand-appearance-settings-overview.png)
### Logos and favicon

Three upload rows sit at the top of the page:

**Full Logo**: your wide logo, shown across the dashboard and portal. Upload an SVG or PNG at least 240px wide.

**Small Logo**: a square version used where space is tight. Upload a square image at least 64px.

**Favicon**: the icon shown in a browser tab. Upload an ICO or PNG, 32px.

Click **Upload** on any row and choose a file. Each logo updates as soon as it uploads, there's no separate save step for logos.

### Primary color

The primary color field sets the accent color used on the applicant portal, booking pages, and portal buttons.

Enter a hex code directly, or click the color swatch to pick one visually. The swatch next to the field updates live as you type.

![Primary color field with a hex value entered and Save enabled](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-your-brand-appearance/brand-appearance-color-picker.png)
Once you've made a change, **Save** becomes active. Click **Reset** to discard your edit and return to the last saved color.

### Preview

The Preview panel on the right shows how your primary color looks on the applicant portal, a booking card, and buttons, updating as you type.

If the color you pick is too light, FS Ai warns you underneath the preview: light colors make white button text hard to read. Pick a darker shade to clear the warning, since **Save** stays disabled while it's showing.

![Preview panel showing the too-light color warning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-set-your-brand-appearance/brand-appearance-preview-warning.png)
