For brands on the funnel surface, Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) shows exactly where each lead stands in your funnel: which step they've reached, whether they're still moving, and what happened when they stopped.

> This applies to brands on the funnel surface (lite brands, and full-plan brands set to the funnel surface). Brands on the applicant portal surface see a Steps Completed field instead.

### See a lead's step in the leads table

Go to **Sales → Pipeline**. Funnel-surface brands show a **Step** column between Status and Email, naming the furthest step each lead has reached.

A colored dot marks where things stand:

- **Green**: the lead finished the funnel or booked a call
- **Blue**: the lead is still moving through the funnel
- **Gray**: the lead hasn't started, or their journey ended before finishing

Hover the step for a one-line summary of what that status means.

![Leads table with the Step column visible, showing a mix of green, blue, and gray step values](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-track-funnel-progress-on-a-lead/leads-table-step-column.png)
### Open the full progress rail

Click a lead with funnel activity to open their detail panel. Below the header, a branch-style rail shows their entire journey: filled dots and solid lines mark the steps they've reached, dashed lines mark track they haven't.

At the qualification gate, the rail splits into two labeled lanes, **Qualified** and **Needs review**. Only one lane fills in, so you can see at a glance which path the lead took.

Hover any dot for a timestamp.

A caption under the rail summarizes their state in plain language, for example **Stopped at Pre-Qualify**. If the lead stalled before finishing, you'll also see a **Send resume link** button to re-send them their funnel link.

Leads with no funnel activity yet don't show a rail, just a **Step: Not started** field.

![Lead detail panel showing the branch-style funnel progress rail mid-funnel, with the Send resume link button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/how-to-track-funnel-progress-on-a-lead/lead-panel-progress-rail-instep.png)
### What the rail shows once a lead is done

When a lead finishes, the caption reflects exactly where they landed:

- **Booked a call**
- **Finished the funnel**
- **Awaiting FDD approval** or **Awaiting signature**
- **FDD request denied**
- **Reached the end**

At this point the resume link button is gone. There's nowhere left to resume into.
