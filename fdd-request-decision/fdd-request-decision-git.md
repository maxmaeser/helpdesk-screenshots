Review held FDD requests in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) from the lead's detail panel. You can approve a request and email a signing link, or deny it while keeping the option to approve later.

> These controls appear for leads with an FDD request from your brand's funnel.

### Find waiting requests

1. Go to **Sales → Pipeline**. On FranDev (Recruitment Funnel), open **Leads** instead.
2. Select **Needs FDD approval** above the leads table.
3. Click the lead you want to review. Their panel opens on the **Details** tab.

![The Needs FDD approval quick view and a waiting lead](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fdd-request-decision/fdd-request-queue.png)

The progress card shows **FDD requested**. Hover over that status to see when it was requested and **Awaiting your decision**.

![The lead's funnel progress card with approval and denial actions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fdd-request-decision/fdd-request-card.png)

Your funnel's qualification rules determine which requests need review. See *[About the Funnel Builder]* for the setup.

If the queue shows **You're all caught up**, no requests are waiting for a decision.

### Approve and send

1. Click **Approve & Send FDD** on the progress card.
2. In **Approve and send the FDD**, choose the state document to send. The choices reflect the lead's recorded states.
3. Read any warnings, then click **Approve & Send FDD** in the dialog.

![The approval dialog with a state document selected](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fdd-request-decision/fdd-request-approve.png)

The applicant receives an email with a signing link. The progress card changes to **Awaiting signature** until the lead completes signing.

A missing document can disable sending. Resolve the problem shown in the dialog before continuing.

### Deny a request

1. Click **Deny** on the progress card.
2. Read **Deny this FDD request?**, then click **Deny request**.

![The confirmation explaining what happens when a pending request is denied](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fdd-request-decision/fdd-request-deny.png)

For a pending request, the applicant does not receive the FDD and stays on their pending screen. The card shows **Request denied**.

You can still use **Approve & Send FDD** on that lead later.

![A denied request with the approval action still available](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fdd-request-decision/fdd-request-denied.png)

### If a signing link was already sent

Denying stops future clicks on the signing link from working. It cannot recall the delivered email.

If the lead already opened the link and the document was served, denying cannot undo that disclosure. Approving again later sends a fresh link.

For the rest of the progress card, see *[How to Track Funnel Progress on a Lead]*.
