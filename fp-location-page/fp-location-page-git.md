Your location page brings together one unit's details, updates, conversations, and files. It's where you can check what's happening at that location and stay in touch with your franchise team.

Open **Locations** and choose a unit. If you operate one unit, **Location** opens its page directly. Your brand may use a different name for the sidebar item.

![Locations navigation and a unit ready to open](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-navigation.png)
## Overview

**Overview** shows the unit's property name, format, building type, square footage, and opening date. If there isn't an actual opening date yet, you may see an estimated date.

**Assigned Brand Representatives** lists your contacts at corporate. **Send Message** opens a private conversation with that person; **Send Email** opens your email app.

The recent activity section shows up to six events.

![Location overview with property facts and assigned representatives](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-overview.png)
## Details

**Details** holds the location record, along with property, lease, compliance, and construction information.

Whether you can edit the record depends on how your brand sets it up. If the fields are read-only, contact your franchise team about corrections.

![Location record and fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-details.png)
## Activity

**Activity** is the running log of updates to your unit. It gives you a place to review changes over time.

If there aren't any entries yet, the page says that updates to your location appear here.

![Location activity with recent updates](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-activity.png)
## Chat

**Chat** holds the conversation linked to this location. Keep unit-specific questions and updates together with the message history.

![Location conversation and message composer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-chat.png)
## Assets

**Assets** holds files shared with this location and the unit's own documents, such as its lease or permits. Available collections and files depend on what your brand has shared and what is on the location record.

![Location assets with collections and location documents](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-assets.png)
On a phone, the tab row scrolls horizontally so you can reach the remaining tabs.

![Location page at phone width](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-location-page/fp-location-mobile.png)
