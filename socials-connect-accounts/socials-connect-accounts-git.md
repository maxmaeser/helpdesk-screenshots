Connect the accounts your brand posts from in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). You can link a new account to locations during setup so the right units have access to it.

> You need Socials enabled for a Full OS brand, Marketing write access, and permission to authorize the account on its social platform.

### Choose the platform

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Socials** and select **Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect Account**.
3. Choose the platform: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, or YouTube.
4. Click **Continue**.

![Platform picker for a new social account](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-connect-accounts/socials-connect-platform.png)

### Link locations and authorize

1. On **Link to locations**, select the locations this account posts for. Use **Search locations** to find a unit.
2. Leave the selection empty if you want to connect the account without linking locations yet.
3. Click **Connect**, then complete the platform's sign-in and authorization screens.
4. Return to **Accounts** and check that the channel appears as **Connected**.

![Optional location selection before connecting](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-connect-accounts/socials-connect-locations.png)

The location choices apply when the connection completes. You can change the account's links later from the location's **Connections** tab.

### Check the connection

The accounts view groups channels by account owner or platform. A channel can belong to corporate, a person, or a location.

Check the connection status before relying on an account for a post. A channel that needs reauthorization must be reconnected before normal activity resumes.

![Connected channels and their account owners](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-connect-accounts/socials-connect-result.png)

Google Business Profile listings may also appear in account management for reviews. They are connected through a location's reputation tools and are not offered in this social publishing picker.
