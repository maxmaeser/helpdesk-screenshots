Manage your team's bookable meetings from the brand settings in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). Individual events use one host's calendar; group events bring several hosts into the same meeting.

> This page requires Full OS. Connect your calendar before creating an individual event. See *[How to Connect Your Calendar]*.

### Open Brand Scheduling

Open **Scheduling** under Brand in your settings. You reach **Settings** from your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar.

The page has **Scheduling events** for individual hosts and **Group scheduling** for meetings with several team members.

![Brand scheduling with individual events and funnel booking checks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-overview.png)

### Create an Individual Event

1. In **Scheduling events**, click **Create event**.
2. Enter the subject and a duration in minutes. The duration must be a multiple of five.
3. Choose the **Calendar** that should receive bookings. This choice is fixed after creation.

![Individual event subject, calendar, and duration fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-event-details.png)

4. Set the **Operating timezone**, check the days you are available, and adjust each day's start and end times.
5. Click **Create**.

![Weekly availability and the Create button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-event-availability.png)

The list brings together individual events from your brand's agents. An **Edit** button opens an event's settings.

### Check Funnel Booking Eligibility

On funnel brands, **Funnel bookable** means the host passes the booking checks. **Not funnel bookable** appears with a reason when the host cannot be booked.

A host needs sales assignment for this brand and a connected calendar. Every member of a group needs both.

The warning's **Manage sales assignment** button opens the brand's team settings. For a missing calendar connection, see *[How to Connect Your Calendar]*.

### Create a Group Event

1. In **Group scheduling**, click **New group event**.
2. Click **Add agent** and choose each team member.
3. Enter a subject and a duration in multiples of five.

![New group event with the member picker open](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-group-members.png)

4. Set the **Operating timezone** and weekly availability, then click **Save**.

![Group event availability and Save button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-group-availability.png)

Group scheduling is collective: every member must be free, and the meeting is booked on every member's own connected calendar.

Use **Manage** to reopen a group. Its row menu also offers **Delete**, with confirmation.

For applicant portal brands, the group menu includes **Copy booking link**. Individual event rows have a link icon for the same purpose. These booking links are not shown for funnel brands.

![Applicant portal brand group menu and individual booking link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-scheduling/brand-scheduling-group-menu.png)
