Creative Editor is your portal's workspace for images, PDFs, and slides. It uses your brand's colors and fonts when your brand has provided them.

Your designs belong to your franchise team. If the page says you haven't been added to a team yet, contact your brand.

## Start a design

1. Open **Studio** in the sidebar, then **Creative Editor**.
2. Click **New Design**.

![Creative Editor in the Lumon Cafe portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-creative-editor/fp-creative-editor-designs.png)
3. Enter a **Design name** in the header.
4. Choose a text preset such as **Title** from the left **Add** panel to put text on the canvas.

![Portal Creative Editor with a text element added](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-creative-editor/fp-creative-editor-canvas.png)
The Add panel also holds shapes, stickers, and available images. **Layers** lists the elements in your design. **Properties** contains controls for the selected element or page.

## Save and return later

5. Click **Save** in the header.
6. Check the **Name**, choose a **File type**, and click **Save** in the dialog.

![Portal design save dialog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-creative-editor/fp-creative-editor-save.png)
PNG and JPEG are for one-page designs. PDF and Slides can hold multiple pages. Your saved design appears on the Creative Editor page, where you can open its card to edit it again.

## Download a finished file

Click **Export**, choose the file type and pages, and leave the destination on **Download**. Click the download button at the bottom of the dialog.

![Portal design export dialog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/fp-creative-editor/fp-creative-editor-export.png)
You can export PNG, JPEG, PDF, or **PowerPoint**. Review any PowerPoint conversion notes before downloading.

The header's **Import** control starts a new design from an image, PDF, or PowerPoint file. Save your current design first if you want to keep it.
