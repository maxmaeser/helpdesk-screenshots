Chat is where you talk with your brand team and the people you work with, right inside the portal instead of scattered across email and text.

## Finding your chats

Click **Chat** in the sidebar. A badge next to it shows how many unread conversations you have.

**Chat Overview** lists every conversation you're part of, newest first. Each row shows who's talking, a preview of the last message, and how long ago it landed. A blue badge on a row means there's something new to read.

![Chat Overview list of conversations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/17-chat/chat-overview-list.png)
Conversations come in a few shapes:

- **Location chats**: tied to one of your units, marked with a pin icon.
- **Group chats**: everyone on a shared thread, marked with a building or people icon.
- **Ticket chats**: the back-and-forth on a ticket you sent through *[Submitting a Ticket]*. Those live here too, right alongside your other conversations.

## Starting a new chat

Click **New Chat** at the top of the list. Pick **New Private Chat** for a one-on-one, or **New Group Chat** to bring in more than one person.

![New Chat dropdown with New Private Chat and New Group Chat options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/17-chat/chat-new-chat-menu.png)
## Reading a conversation

Open any row to see the full thread. The header shows who you're talking to and, for a group, how many members are in it.

Each message shows the sender's name, their message, and the time it was sent. Your own messages line up on the right.

![An open chat thread with messages and reactions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/17-chat/chat-thread-view.png)
Hover a message to react to it, or reply underneath with your own message.

## Sending a message

Type in the **Write a message...** box at the bottom of the thread. Use the paperclip to attach a photo or file, the smiley to drop in an emoji, or the microphone for a voice message.

![Message composer with attachment, emoji, and voice options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/17-chat/chat-composer.png)
Press **Send** when you're ready.
