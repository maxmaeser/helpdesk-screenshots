Social Analytics in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) shows how connected social accounts and their posts perform. Go to **Marketing** → **Socials** and select **Analytics**.

> This page is available for Full OS brands with Socials enabled and Marketing read access. Figures depend on the connected accounts and the analytics data available for them.

### Set the reporting scope

The filter bar sets the date range, account groups, and platforms included in the report. Keep that scope in mind when comparing totals or checking a single location's results.

The summary cards cover **Impressions**, **Reach**, **Engagement Rate**, **Net Follower Growth**, **Link Clicks**, and **Video Views**.

![Reporting filters and social performance summary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-analytics/socials-analytics-summary.png)

### Read the trends

**Performance Over Time** plots the selected metric by day. The **vs Previous Period** control adds the comparison period to the chart.

**Channel Performance** breaks results down by platform. **Account Leaderboard** compares individual accounts, including follower growth and change against the prior period.

Selecting a channel or account row focuses the dashboard on it. The selected filters explain why the report may show a smaller set of results.

![Performance trend with the previous-period comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-analytics/socials-analytics-trend.png)

### Review posts and audiences

**Top Posts** ranks content for the selected period. Its metric selector supports engagements, impressions, link clicks, and engagement rate.

**Audience Snapshot** summarizes available demographic data. The empty-state guidance identifies Instagram and YouTube as sources for these audience figures once synchronization completes.

![Top-performing posts for the reporting period](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/socials-analytics/socials-analytics-posts.png)

An empty section means the report has no data to show for that scope. It does not establish that a new account or post has had no activity. Allow synchronized data to arrive before interpreting a missing result.
