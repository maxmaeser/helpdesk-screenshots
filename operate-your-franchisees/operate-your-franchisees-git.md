Operate is the stage after the deal is signed, when a prospect becomes a franchisee you actually run in Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai).

You are in this stage once sites are opening and your franchisees need the location records, files, and documents that keep the day-to-day running.

![Overview of the Operate section](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/operate-your-franchisees/operate-your-franchisees-overview-v3-r2026-10.png)

Everything below lives under the **Operations** section of the sidebar, where you track each location and manage the assets you share out to the field.

![operate-your-franchisees opener](https://pub-0b63cab43f8b4fe4a9ede117b14f750c.r2.dev/operate-your-franchisees/operate-your-franchisees-opener-v5.png)

### What to do

1. [About Locations](/helpdesk/about-locations): track every site your franchise operates or plans to open, from status and franchisee entity to the details behind each one.

2. [How to Upload and Manage Assets](/helpdesk/how-to-upload-and-manage-assets): add the PDFs, images, videos, and documents your franchisees need, and keep them versioned and organized in one library.

3. [How Assets Reach Your Franchisees](/helpdesk/how-assets-reach-your-franchisees): control which franchisees can see an asset through its destination library or collection.

> Visibility follows the destination library and collection permissions. A file uploaded directly into a location's shared library can be visible to that location's franchisees immediately; an upload to your private library still needs to be shared. See [How Assets Reach Your Franchisees](/helpdesk/how-assets-reach-your-franchisees).
