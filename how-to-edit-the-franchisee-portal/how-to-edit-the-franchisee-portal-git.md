The Franchisee Portal editor controls what franchisees see when they log into their own portal.

You set a hero image, choose a top bar color, and decide which sidebar items appear and in what order.

> **Prerequisite:** You need Studio access on the brand. The Franchisee Portal editor is only available on full-plan brands with a connected portal domain.

### Opening the Franchisee Portal Editor

Go to **Studio → Franchisee Portal** in the sidebar.

On brands where this item appears, it skips the Studio hub and opens the editor directly.

The editor splits into two halves. Your controls sit in a panel on the left: **Hero image**, **Top bar color**, and **Sidebar**.

A live preview of the portal sits on the right, and it updates as you make changes.

### Setting the Hero Image

Click **Upload image** to add a background image for the franchisee portal's home hero.

Once an image is set, the button becomes **Replace image**. Click **Reset to default** to remove your image.

### Setting the Top Bar Color

Use the color picker under **Top bar color** to set the background color of the top bar franchisees see across every page of their portal.

Click **Reset to default** to remove your custom color. Without one, the top bar falls back to your brand's primary color.

### Reordering and Showing or Hiding Sidebar Items

The **Sidebar** section controls the navigation franchisees see inside their own portal.

Drag an item by its handle to reorder it. Use the toggle on the right to show or hide it.

You can't rename these items. Each one keeps the label FS Ai assigns it.

A few items are locked. **Home** always sits first and can't be hidden.

**Locations**, **Team**, and **Chat** can't be turned off.

An item marked **Coming soon** has its toggle disabled because that feature isn't ready to ship to franchisees yet.

Click **Reset to default** to restore the original order and visibility for every item.

### Publishing Your Changes

None of your changes take effect until you click **Publish** in the top right.

**View Live Portal** opens the last published version, not your unsaved edits. Check the preview panel here for anything you haven't published yet.

Click **Exit Without Saving** to leave the editor. If you have unsaved changes, FS Ai asks you to confirm before discarding them.
