Your brand voice tells Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) how to sound when it writes for you. Add voices for different audiences and choose one as the default.

> You need permission to edit brand settings to create or manage voices.

### Open brand voice settings

1. Click your profile picture at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings**.

![Profile menu with Settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-navigation.png)

2. Open the **Profile** page under Brand.

![Brand Profile settings link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-profile.png)

3. Find **Brand voice** and click **Set up your brand voice**.

![Brand voice card before the first voice is created](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-empty.png)

### Answer the guided questions

1. Under **Tone**, choose the words that fit your emails. Add any extra guidance in **Anything else about the tone? (optional)**, or skip this step. Click **Continue** between questions.

![Tone choices and optional guidance in the setup dialog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-tone.png)

2. Answer **How do you talk in this voice?** This answer is required. Describe how you address readers and the language you use.

![Required description of how the brand talks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-how.png)

3. Use **Anything you never say?** for words or claims to avoid. You can leave it blank.

![Optional words and phrasing to avoid](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-never.png)

4. Add **Phrases that sound like us** and **How we sign off**, if useful.

![Example phrases and an email sign-off](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-phrases.png)

5. Review your answers, then click **Save Brand Voice**. Your first voice is named Brand voice automatically and becomes the default.

![First voice summary and Save Brand Voice button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-review.png)

After saving, FS Ai generates a sample email. The default guides new AI-written emails; saving a voice does not rewrite existing content.

### Add and manage voices

1. Click **Add a voice** on the card to create another style.

![Saved voices with the default badge and Add a voice button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-list.png)

2. Give it a name under **What should we call this voice?** and optionally describe **When do we use this voice? (optional)**. Continue through the same questions.

![Name and intended use for an additional voice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-name.png)

3. Review your answers and click **Save Voice**.

![Additional voice summary and Save Voice button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-add-review.png)

Open a voice's three-dot menu for **Edit**, **Set as default**, or **Archive**. The default carries a **Default** badge.

![Menu for a voice that is not the default](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-menu.png)

After choosing **Archive**, confirm with **Archive voice**. To archive the current default, set another voice as the default first.

![Confirmation before archiving a voice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/brand-voice/brand-voice-archive.png)

Archiving removes a voice from writing choices and keeps existing content intact.
