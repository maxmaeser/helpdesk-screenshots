Courses let you build training content and track how your team and franchisees move through it.

Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai) organizes courses, enrollments, and results in one place under **Operations > Courses**.

### Stat tiles

Four stat tiles sit at the top of the page and stay visible across every tab:

- **Total Courses**
- **Published**
- **Enrollments**
- **Completion Rate**

### Courses tab

The **Courses** tab is the default view. It lists every course in a table with these columns: **Course**, **Category**, **Status**, **Author**, **Duration**, and **Created**.

Clicking into a course from the table opens it in the course builder, where you assemble the course content.

![Courses tab with stat tiles and course table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-courses/courses-stat-tiles.png)

### Learning Paths tab

The **Learning Paths** tab groups multiple courses into a sequence a learner completes in order.

This tab starts empty, showing "No Learning Paths Yet" until you create your first one.

![Learning Paths tab showing the No Learning Paths Yet empty state](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-courses/courses-learning-paths-empty.png)

### Certificates tab

The **Certificates** tab holds the certificates learners earn for completing courses or learning paths.

This tab also starts empty, showing "No Certificates Yet" until you create your first one.

![Certificates tab showing the No Certificates Yet empty state](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-courses/courses-certificates-empty.png)

### Enrollments tab

The **Enrollments** tab tracks who has been assigned to what. It lists each enrollment in a table with these columns: **Assignment**, **Franchisee**, **Type**, **Status**, **Progress**, **Due Date**, and **Assigned**.

![Enrollments tab listing assignments with franchisee, type, status, and progress](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-courses/courses-enrollments.png)

### Analytics tab

The **Analytics** tab breaks down performance across three sub-tabs: **Overview**, **Course**, and **Franchisee**.

Across these sub-tabs you'll find cards covering **Enrolments**, **Completion Rate**, **Active Learners**, **Learning Hours**, **Quiz Performance**, and **Course Library**.

Use this tab to see how your training is landing, both across your whole program and broken down by individual course or franchisee.

![Analytics Overview sub-tab with enrolment, completion, and quiz performance cards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/about-courses/courses-analytics-overview.png)
