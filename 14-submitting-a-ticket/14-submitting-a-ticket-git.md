When something comes up in your unit that needs your brand's help, you can submit a ticket.

Brand requests go to your brand team, while **Bug or technical issue** reports go to Franchise Systems Ai (FS Ai). You track replies in the portal instead of losing them in email.

## Finding the help menu

The **Need help?** button sits at the top right of the portal, marked with a question bubble icon.

Click it for a short menu:

- **Submit a ticket**: start a new ticket.
- **Open Helpdesk**: browse these guides if you'd rather look something up first.

## Submitting a ticket

Click **Submit a ticket** and a short flow walks you through four steps:

1. **Type**: pick the category that fits your issue. When your brand enables brand requests, its categories and **Something else** send requests to your brand team. **Bug or technical issue** sends a portal problem to FS Ai and is available even when brand requests are off.
2. **Details**: add a short **Subject** and a **Description** of what's going on.
3. **Attachments**: drag in screenshots, photos, or short videos. For **Bug or technical issue** reports, the portal captures the page behind the ticket window when you reach this step and attaches it for you. Remove it if you'd rather not share it.
4. **Review**: check it over and press **Submit**. You'll get a confirmation that it's on its way.

## Tracking your tickets

Open **Tickets** in the sidebar to see every ticket you've submitted. Your brand can rename or hide this sidebar item.

Filter along the top by category, and open any ticket to message back and forth with the team handling it, like a conversation.

Each ticket carries a status so you always know where it stands:

- **Open**: submitted and waiting to be picked up.
- **In progress**: the team handling the ticket is working on it.
- **Waiting on you**: they need something from you to move forward.
- **Resolved**: handled.
- **Closed**: wrapped up.
