When something comes up in your unit that needs your brand's help, you can submit a ticket.

It goes straight to your brand team, and you track their reply in the portal instead of losing it in email.

## Finding the help menu

The **Need help?** button sits at the top right of the portal, marked with a question bubble icon.

Click it for a short menu:

- **Submit a ticket**: start a new ticket.
- **My tickets**: see everything you've sent and your brand's replies.
- **Open Helpdesk**: browse these guides if you'd rather look something up first.

![Need help? menu open with Submit a ticket, My tickets, and Open Helpdesk](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxmaeser/helpdesk-screenshots/master/14-submitting-a-ticket/ticket-help-menu.png)
## Submitting a ticket

Click **Submit a ticket** and a short flow walks you through four steps:

1. **Type**: pick the category that fits your issue. Your brand sets up these categories, so what you see depends on how they've set it up. There's also **Something else** for a general request, and **Bug or technical issue** for a problem with the portal itself.
2. **Details**: add a short **Subject** and a **Description** of what's going on.
3. **Attachments**: drag in screenshots, photos, or short videos. When you start a ticket from the **Need help?** button, the portal grabs a picture of the page you were on and attaches it for you. Remove it if you'd rather not share it.
4. **Review**: check it over and press **Submit**. You'll get a confirmation that it's on its way.

## Tracking your tickets

Open **My tickets** from the same menu to see every ticket you've submitted.

Filter along the top by category, and open any ticket to message back and forth with your brand team, like a conversation.

Each ticket carries a status so you always know where it stands:

- **Open**: submitted and waiting to be picked up.
- **In progress**: your brand team is working on it.
- **Waiting on you**: they need something from you to move forward.
- **Resolved**: handled.
- **Closed**: wrapped up.
